Your thinking space.

Talk to your assistant and ask it to write or draw on WhiteBoard. Its MCP connection sends updates directly to this canvas.

Try: “Show three apples, then add two more.” Or: “Draw a parabola and label its vertex.”

You can also draw with the pen, add text or LaTeX equations, and undo edits. All changes are shared with the assistant. Save SVG exports a static copy; equations require a browser that supports SVG foreignObject.

This prototype has no microphone or AI service of its own. Voice runs in your assistant app. Voice-to-tool support must be tested there. Drawing animations are not synchronized to spoken words.
